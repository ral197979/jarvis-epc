## 1. Facility Manager

**Profile.** Owns the building or facility once it's turned over. Inherits whatever the project team hands them — and lives with the gaps.

**Daily responsibilities**
- Keep the facility running and safe
- Maintain the asset register and operating documentation
- Coordinate maintenance, inspections, and compliance
- Respond to issues and answer "where is the record for…?"

**Pain points**
- Receives a chaotic handover — boxes of PDFs, no structured asset data
- No reliable test history or as-built record for the systems they now own
- Tribal knowledge walks out with the project team
- Compliance and inspection obligations are tracked manually

**How the platform helps**
- Inherits a **complete commissioning and turnover record** — every asset's test history, deficiencies, and documents
- A structured **asset register** (system → subsystem → tag) instead of a binder
- **Ask Jarvis** turns the entire document corpus into instant, cited answers ("what's the startup sequence for chiller 2?")
- **Compliance** tracking with evidence and overdue flagging
- **Asset health scoring** based on inspection, deficiency, and incident history

**What success looks like.** Day-one operational readiness with a searchable, trustworthy record — no scramble to reconstruct what was built.

## 2. Operations / Project Manager

**Profile.** Runs active projects day to day. The person everyone asks "what's the status?"

**KPIs**
- On-time and on-budget delivery (SPI/CPI)
- Open vs. closed actions; SLA compliance
- RFI/submittal turnaround
- Readiness toward milestones

**Pain points**
- Status lives in a dozen tools and spreadsheets; reconciliation eats the day
- Finds out about problems too late to fix cheaply
- Commitments slip through email cracks
- Reporting to leadership is manual and stale

**How the platform helps**
- **One source of truth** across the project lifecycle
- **Action Center** with SLA, escalation, and real-time notifications — nothing dropped
- **Predict** flags slippage early with health scores and EAC forecasts
- **Dashboard** and **Ops Command Center** replace manual status assembly
- Real-time data means leadership reporting is a click, not a project

**What success looks like.** A project run from one screen, with problems caught early and reporting that builds itself.

## 3. Maintenance / Reliability Manager

**Profile.** Responsible for keeping assets reliable and minimizing unplanned downtime after turnover.

**KPIs**
- Asset availability / uptime
- Mean time to resolution
- Backlog of corrective actions
- Recurring-failure rate

**Pain points**
- No structured history of how each asset was commissioned or what failed before
- Repeated troubleshooting of the same problems
- Reactive rather than predictive maintenance posture
- Knowledge locked in a few experienced heads

**How the platform helps**
- **Asset health scoring** from inspection history, open deficiencies, incident frequency, and age, with ranked recommendations
- **Fix Library** of reusable resolution patterns mined from deficiency history
- **Ask Jarvis** for instant, cited answers from manuals and procedures
- **Action Center** for tracked corrective work with SLA and escalation
- **Root-cause correlation** to understand *why* something failed

**What success looks like.** Fewer repeat failures, faster resolution, and a maintenance posture informed by real asset history.

## 4. Utility / Plant Owner's Representative

**Profile.** Represents the owner (a utility, municipality, or operator) accepting a delivered facility — a water/wastewater plant, a power facility, an industrial process unit.

**KPIs**
- Acceptance on schedule with no open critical deficiencies
- Completeness of the turnover package
- Warranty and O&M readiness
- Risk transferred cleanly at handover

**Pain points**
- Can't objectively tell if the facility is genuinely ready to accept
- Commissioning records are inconsistent and hard to verify
- Disputes over what was tested and what's outstanding
- Risk of accepting a facility that isn't operationally ready

**How the platform helps**
- An objective, weighted **readiness score** with the exact blocking factors
- A **defensible commissioning record** — every test, witness, and evidence link
- **Deficiency and retest** tracking that proves issues were actually resolved
- A complete **turnover package** (O&M, spares, training, warranty)
- **IoT sensor monitoring** with threshold alerts during commissioning

**What success looks like.** A confident, evidence-backed acceptance decision — accept when the number says ready, with the proof on file.

> **Honest scope.** The platform proves the plant is *ready to operate* and hands over the record. It is not the SCADA/DCS that *operates* the running plant day to day — it integrates with that operational-technology layer.

## 5. Commissioning Authority (CxA)

**Profile.** The independent (or owner-side) authority responsible for verifying that every system performs as designed. The platform's most enthusiastic champion.

**KPIs**
- Test completion rate by phase
- Deficiency closure rate and aging
- Schedule adherence of the Cx plan
- Quality and defensibility of the commissioning record

**Pain points**
- Running commissioning on paper checklists, spreadsheets, and PDFs
- Lost or inconsistent test records
- Manual tracking of deficiencies and retests
- No real-time view of readiness across systems
- Reconstructing the record for disputes or audits

**How the platform helps**
- A structured **four-phase workflow** across **21 asset types** with phase-specific templates
- **Test packs** with per-step expected/actual, performed-by, witnessed-by, and evidence
- **Deficiency management** with a retest loop that closes only on a passing retest
- **Readiness scoring** at project/system/subsystem level with blocking factors and history
- **Coverage reporting** to ensure every tag is tested
- Downloadable, audit-ready **turnover deliverables**

**What success looks like.** Commissioning run as a controlled, evidence-based process — defensible, real-time, and dramatically faster to close out.

## 6. EPC Project Manager

**Profile.** Accountable for delivering the project on scope, schedule, and budget — and protecting margin through change.

**KPIs**
- CPI / SPI and margin at completion
- Change-order recovery
- RFI/submittal cycle time
- On-time turnover

**Pain points**
- Cost and schedule data lag reality
- Disputed and unrecovered change orders erode margin
- Coordination gaps between engineering, field, and commissioning
- Painful, manual close-out and turnover

**How the platform helps**
- **Earned Value Management** and **Cost Control** with real-time CPI/SPI and forecast-at-completion
- **Change Orders** priced, approved, and tracked to protect margin
- Unified **engineering coordination** (drawings, RFIs, submittals, BIM)
- **Commissioning** that drives a clean, on-time turnover
- **Predict** for early warning before overruns compound

**What success looks like.** Margin protected, changes recovered, and turnover delivered on schedule with a clean record.

## 7. Executive Leadership

**Profile.** C-suite and program directors accountable for the portfolio's financial and operational performance.

**KPIs**
- Portfolio margin, IRR/NPV/MOIC
- Projects at risk
- Cash and AR exposure
- Delivery predictability

**Pain points**
- No single, trustworthy portfolio view
- Surprises — overruns surface in monthly reviews, too late
- Hard to know where to focus limited attention
- Can't quantify the ROI of operational improvements

**How the platform helps**
- **Executive Command Center** — portfolio risk heatmap, SLA, escalation hotspots, contractor performance
- **Predict** portfolio health, EAC forecasts, and anomaly alerts
- **Portfolio** financials with IRR/NPV/MOIC and scenario modeling
- Real-time, drill-down-able truth — not stale reports
- Auditability and compliance evidence by default

**ROI (summary).** Fewer overruns, faster turnover, lower toolchain cost, reduced compliance effort, and protected margin. Worked examples for embassies, data centers, hospitals, universities, water plants, and industrial facilities are in `ROI_ANALYSIS.md`.

**What success looks like.** A portfolio leadership can see, trust, and steer in real time — with the numbers to prove the investment.
