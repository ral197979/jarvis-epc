# Denver Engineering — Customer Features

> **The AI-native project operating system for capital construction.**
> A customer-facing tour of what the platform does. Organized by the value it delivers,
> not the code behind it. Every module listed below is live in the application.

Denver Engineering combines a complete construction **system of record** with an
**AI intelligence layer** that reads your live project data and tells you what to do
about it — what's at risk, why, and what to do next — with the math shown, not hidden.

---

## 1. AI Project Intelligence

The differentiator. These read live signals across every module and turn them into decisions.

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| 🧭 **Focus** | Your morning briefing: "what should I focus on today?" Ranks overdue RFIs, stalled submittals, high risks, failed inspections, hot punch items, blocked work, budget overruns, and schedule slip into one prioritized list — each with a plain-English reason, a recommended action, and a one-click deep-link to the record. |
| 🔗 **Coordination** | Surfaces where the project is blocked or out of sync: missing approvals, dependency blockers, out-of-sequence schedule clashes, open BIM clashes, and pending change orders. |
| 📋 **Executive** | Board-level portfolio health — a health score per project, status mix, systemic issues recurring across projects, and a worst-first project table. Generates a **copy-pasteable owner/board report** in one click. |
| 🗂️ **Portfolio IQ** | Compares projects: benchmarks (cost variance, schedule slip, overdue work), **resource conflicts** (people over-allocated across projects), exemplars to replicate, and outliers needing attention. |
| 🤖 **Autopilot** | Autonomous coordination — monitors signals, detects breaks, and recommends actions with owners and due dates. Nothing is executed without human approval; every decision is logged. |
| 🔮 **Predict** | Portfolio health forecasting — red/amber/green risk, CPI/SPI trends, EAC projection, and anomaly flags. |
| 💬 **Ask Jarvis** | A grounded AI assistant that answers from your ingested project documents with citations — never hallucinated. |

> **Why it's trustworthy:** the rankings and scores are produced by deterministic, unit-tested logic — auditable and explainable, not a black box. AI is used for language, not for the numbers.

## 3. Schedule

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| 📅 **Import Schedule** | Drag-and-drop Primavera P6 (XER) and MS Project (XML) with CPM/baseline parsing. |
| 🎲 **Schedule Forecast** | Monte Carlo completion forecast (P10/P50/P80/P90), probability of hitting a target date, task **criticality index**, the critical path, a **recovery planner**, and a **what-if** simulator ("delay this task N days → new finish"). |

## 4. Coordination — RFIs, Submittals, Drawings, BIM

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| ❓ **RFIs** | Request-for-information log with review and overdue tracking. The **RFI Copilot** answers "has this been asked before?", suggests who should respond, and shows schedule impact. |
| 📨 **Submittals** | Shop-drawing/product-data workflow. The **review assistant** runs readiness checks, finds precedent submittals, suggests a reviewer, and flags deviation risk. |
| 📐 **Drawings** | Sheet register by discipline with revisions and red-line markups. |
| 🏢 **BIM** | IFC model upload with clash/coordination issue tracking. |
| 📬 **Transmittals** | Formal document transmittal workflow with response tracking. |
| 🗄️ **Documents** | Upload PDFs/Office/CSV/images/3D models with full-text + AI-summarized search. |
| 📡 **IoT Sensors** | Sensor registry, live readings, time-series charts, and alert thresholds. |

## 5. Quality & Safety

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---|---|
